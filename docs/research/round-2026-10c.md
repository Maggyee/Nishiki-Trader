# Research Round 2026-10c (intraday)

- **Status**: Closed 2026-10-20. v60 failed development; see Results.
- **Goal**: Profit after costs from minute-level data, under
  [ADR-018](../decisions/018-intraday-research.md): positions held at least
  four hours, one-minute execution lag, spot costs 12/20 bps per fill, a
  timing null shifted by whole days, ≥ 60 positions and ≥ 60 active days.
- **Operator choice**: of two proposals (cascade rebound; BTC-led altcoin
  catch-up) the operator chose the cascade rebound only.

| Protocol | Contract | Idea | Direction | Windows (dev / conf) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v60 | [cascade rebound](contracts/v60-cascade-rebound.json) | 10 coins: 60-minute spot return and 60-minute perp OI change both in the bottom 0.5% of the trailing 30 days → long spot 4 hours | long/flat | 2022–23 / 2024–25 |

Knowledge disclosure (in the contract): v60 is the intraday version of v57,
designed after v57 missed development on p (0.105) with 2023 negative. The
2024–25 confirmation is binding; under W3 a development pass alone means little.

Mechanism: `apps/strategies_freqtrade/research/intraday_flow.py`, cross-checked
against an independent reference implementation before any run. The contract's
`semantics` field is the specification.

## Results

Notional 10,000 USDT, 1-minute bars, one-minute execution lag, spot costs
12 bps per fill (stress 20). File: [results/v60-development.json](results/v60-development.json).

| Identity | Window | Gross | Base PnL | Stress | Benchmark | Null p | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v60 `cascade_rebound` | dev 2022–23 | +1,560 | +124 | −832 | +37 | 0.0002 | rejected (stress, 2022 negative, leave-best −215) |

- 598 positions over 250 active days. Costs ate 92% of gross: about 0.26% gross
  per trade against a 0.24% round trip.
- The timing beats random entry clearly (null mean −1,358, p 0.0002), so the
  rebound is real but about one round trip of retail taker costs in size.
- Max drawdown 2,148 USDT (21.5% of notional), mostly on 2022-11-08 (FTX):
  four coins bought into the cascade lost 11–16% in four hours.
- Confirmation 2024–25 was not opened; it stays clean for any later variant.

### Exploratory diagnosis (development window only, not a protocol)

- By year, gross: 2022 −759, 2023 +2,318. Mean move after entry: +25 bps at
  4 h (t 2.0), fading to about 0 by 48 h; 2022 negative at every horizon from
  4 h, 2023 positive at all of them.
- A trap worth remembering: splitting entries by "five or more coins triggered
  that day" made single-coin cascades look strong (+52 bps at 4 h), but the
  day count uses triggers after the entry. With only information at the
  decision minute (other coins already holding), no subset is robust, and
  without XRP on 2023-07-13 (+38% after the court ruling) the low-overlap
  entries earn about 0. Classify events only with what is known at the decision.
- Not rescued (W3). A cheaper venue would change the arithmetic, but the
  effect also depends on the regime (bear-market contagion turns it negative).
