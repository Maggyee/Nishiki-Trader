## Why this round looks different

52 protocols tested directional timing of BTC from public data; none showed
alpha, and the top three backlog ideas (B1–B3) failed under Gates v2. The
program never evaluated **holding the funding carry itself** (long spot, short
perp, collect funding). v50/v51 used funding only as a timing signal, and the
legacy `curve_carry` was a long/flat BTC rule. Carry is a structural premium
paid by leveraged longs, measurable from official immutable archives with no
provider risk. It goes first.

## B9 — Delta-neutral funding carry (new family `funding_carry`)

- **Mechanism**: perp longs pay funding for leverage; holding long spot plus
  an equal-notional short USD-M perp collects funding with near-zero price
  delta.
- **Orthogonality**: market-neutral; earns most in crowded, euphoric long
  phases, when the closed survivors (long BTC in calm regimes) had no edge.
- **Directional capability**: market-neutral, two legs, 1x, no leverage.
- **Universe**: BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT, equal notional, evaluated as one book.
- **Identities** (two, to limit multiplicity):
  - `C1 always-on`: hold both legs continuously; rebalance when leg notionals
    drift > 5%.
  - `C2 filtered`: enter when the trailing 7-day mean funding exceeds the
    cost-recovery rate (round-trip cost over a 30-day hold), exit when the
    trailing 3-day mean turns negative. Thresholds are structural, not tuned.
- **PnL**: funding cash flows + spot leg + perp leg (basis changes) − 4 fills
  per round trip. Return measured on total capital: spot notional + perp
  margin.
- **Gates**: market-neutral class. Benchmark 0. C1: stationary-bootstrap p of
  mean daily PnL ≤ 0.10. C2: random on/off null with the same exposure,
  p ≤ 0.10. Plus base/stress > 0, years ≥ 2/3, months vs null median.
  "≥ 60 positions" is read as ≥ 60 daily observations; this is stated, not hidden.
- **Extra risk gates**: max equity drawdown ≤ 5% of capital; the perp leg must
  never come within 50% of liquidation at 1x using hourly highs; worst
  30-day funding stretch reported.
- **Weekend coverage**: funding settles 24/7 every 8h; no vacuum.
- **If it passes**: stop and ask. Trading it needs a USD-M short hedge leg,
  which ADR-001/ADR-013 currently exclude (spot only), and a two-leg intent
  that `SignalEvent v1` cannot express (ADR-002). Both need new ADRs before any
  testnet work.

## B6 — Taker-flow and open-interest imbalance (family `microstructure_flow`, open)

- **Mechanism**: aggressive flow and leverage build-up predict short-horizon
  BTC moves. Two pre-registered hypotheses, no direction fishing:
  - `F1 taker continuation`: 24h USD-M taker-buy share z-score (90-day
    baseline) > +1 → long next day; < −1 → short next day; else flat.
  - `F2 OI squeeze`: OI up > 5% over 3 days while price is down → long 3 days
    (crowded shorts); mirrored for short.
- **Data**: USD-M klines (taker-buy volume, from 2020); `metrics` OI from
  ~2021-12, so F2 uses an adjusted pre-registered window: development
  2022-01..2023-12, confirmation 2024-01..2025-12.
- **Directional capability**: two-sided in research. Live would be long/flat
  until shorts are allowed; the long-only leg is reported separately.
- **Gates**: directional class. Benchmark = net exposure × B&H; random-timing
  null with the same exposure and holding cadence.
