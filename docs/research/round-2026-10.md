## How a stage runs

`python -m apps.research.protocol run <contract> --stage development|confirmation`

- Data: Binance Vision only, SHA256-checked against the published
  `.CHECKSUM`, cached under `data/research/`. Dates from 2026-09-01 are refused
  (sealed blind).
- Replay: weights decided at a daily close apply to the next day's return.
  Constant notional (10,000 USDT, two-sided, gross ≤ 1). Costs per fill on
  traded notional: base 12 bps, stress 15 bps.
- Null: each asset's position series is circularly shifted against its returns
  (5,000 trials). This keeps exposure, turnover, holding cadence and long/short
  mix, and destroys only timing.
- Gates: base and stress > 0; positive years ≥ 2/3 of window years; positive
  months > null median (§11); ≥ 60 closed positions; leave-best > 0;
  base > benchmark (mean net weight × constant-notional buy-and-hold per
  asset); null p ≤ 0.10.
- Confirmation runs only for identities that passed development.
- Interpretation fixed before any PnL: the contracts write 2/3 as 0.6667, read
  with a 0.001 tolerance (2 of 3 years passes; 2 of 2 needed for 2-year windows).
- v55 amendment before any PnL: USD-M archives start 2020-01, so the fetch
  start moved from 2019-09; rules, windows and gates unchanged.
- Results go to `docs/research/results/`; the registry is updated in the same
  commit.

## Results

Notional 10,000 USDT, base costs 12 bps per fill. Files: [results/](results/).

| Identity | Window | Base PnL | Benchmark | Null p | Outcome |

| v54 `tsmom_vol` | dev 2020–22 | +15,130 | +1,765 | 0.016 | passed |
| v54 `tsmom_vol` | conf 2023–25 | +3,820 | +2,679 | 0.295 | rejected (p, months 16 ≤ 20) |
| v55 `taker_continuation` | dev 2020–22 | +3,417 | +77 | 0.115 | rejected (p) |
| v55 `oi_squeeze` | dev 2022–23 | −5,207 | −107 | 0.760 | rejected (all but activity) |

- v54: profit came from the long side in bull years (2020–21, 2023–24). The
  short side lost in five of six years; 2022 and 2025 were slightly negative.
  Development drawdown 7,617 USDT (24% of equity at the peak), lasting 11 months.
- v55 F1: +9,009 gross, but daily switching cost ~5,600. Near miss on p; under
  W3 it is not rescued. A lower-turnover variant would be a new identity.
- v55 F2: the contrarian squeeze direction lost; flipping it after seeing the
  result would be data snooping (W3).

## Learning simulation (2026-10-18)

The rejected v54 rule, long/flat on spot, run through the real path for
practice: `export_signals` → SignalStore → Nautilus backtest per coin (1h bars,
cash account, fixed quantity worth ~1,000 USDT at 2023-01 prices, 0.1% fee) →
paper runner (BTC, catalog replay) → `report_paper_bundle` → dashboard snapshot.

- Holdings in Nautilus matched the research replay day for day (LTC: 1 day off).
- 2023–25 total +11,588 USDT (2023 +5,659, 2024 +11,312, 2025 −5,382) against
  +20,068 for holding the same quantities; fees 656. Max drawdown 17,753 from
  2024-12-03 to 2025-06-22. Fixed quantities let position value grow to 48,606
  by 2025-08, so late losses outweighed early gains.
- Bug found and fixed (4f9236a): the paper runner traded its instrument on
  other coins' signals from the same stream.
- The paper runtime records fills without fees (BTC +2,294 vs +2,184 in the
  backtest); costs are applied later by `alpha_review`.
