## Results

Notional 10,000 USDT, 1-minute bars, one-minute execution lag, spot costs
12 bps per fill (stress 20). File: [results/v61-development.json](results/v61-development.json).

| Identity | Window | Gross | Base PnL | Stress | Benchmark | Null p | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v61 `laggard_catchup` | dev 2020–22 | +5,877 | −4,083 | −10,723 | +2,281 | 0.075 | rejected (base, stress, years, leave-best, benchmark) |

- About 415 trades of three legs at gross 1: roughly 0.14% gross per trade
  against a 0.24% round trip, so costs turned a small edge into a large loss
  (cumulative drawdown 87% of notional, mostly fees).
- The laggards did catch up a little more than random timing would (p 0.075),
  but by about half a round trip of costs.
- Per the stopping rule set before freezing, the alpha search stops here.
